PicoDeck turns the PicoPad into a tiny app computer.

PicoPad Arcade · RP2040 · Pico W

Games, WiFi and custom apps.
In your pocket.

PicoDeck is the launcher and app platform for the PicoPad. Flash games, run the radio natively, install apps from the SD card or the store, and keep a recovery image that always boots.
